Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get started with NOVO?

Download NOVO from the App Store, open it and follow the short setup: choose what you’re tracking and when you started.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything you enter stays on your device. The app sends usage statistics to Google Firebase, such as which features are used and streak numbers, but never your journal text or what you track. Purchase information goes to RevenueCat. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I sync across multiple devices?

No. NOVO keeps your data on one device and doesn’t sync it.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

In short

What you enter stays on your device, and we can’t see it. NOVO has no accounts. It sends usage statistics to Google Firebase and purchase information to RevenueCat.

What stays on your device

  • What you’re tracking and your start dates
  • Check-ins, difficulty ratings, slips and past journeys
  • Journal entries, goals and coping strategies

What the app sends

  • Usage statistics (Google Firebase Analytics): screens and features used, streak numbers and difficulty ratings, with a random user ID. Never your journal text or what you track.
  • App settings downloaded from Firebase Remote Config
  • Purchase information (Apple and RevenueCat)

No Account

NOVO has no user accounts. Your data stays on your device and we can’t recover it, so keep your device backed up.

Not Medical Advice

NOVO is a self-tracking tool, not medical treatment. It doesn’t replace a doctor, a therapist or a treatment programme. Stopping some substances suddenly can be dangerous, so talk to a doctor first. If you’re in crisis, contact your local emergency number.

Purchases

NOVO offers an auto-renewing subscription and a one-time lifetime purchase through the App Store. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Apple handles refunds.
